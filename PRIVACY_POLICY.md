# Privacy Policy for Animal Match Pop

**Effective Date:** November 10, 2026  
**Developer / Studio:** Pixel Vector  
**Package Name:** `com.pixelvector.animalmatch`

## 1. Overview
Pixel Vector ("we", "our", or "us") built the **Animal Match Pop** app as an ad-supported casual puzzle game with an optional one-time in-app purchase (`remove_ads`).

## 2. Information Collection and Use
We do not directly collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location) to our own servers. All game records (high scores, coins, sound settings) are stored locally on your device using encrypted Android Jetpack DataStore Preferences.

However, the app integrates third-party SDKs provided by Google that may collect device identifiers and diagnostic data:
- **Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK):** Collects Advertising ID (`AD_ID`), coarse location, and ad interaction metrics to serve adaptive banner ads.
- **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP):** Manages GDPR/privacy consent choices for users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK.
- **Google Play Billing:** Processes in-app purchases (`remove_ads`) securely through Google Play. We do not access your payment card details.

## 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA)
This application is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

## 4. Data Security
All network communications initiated by integrated SDKs enforce HTTPS (`usesCleartextTraffic="false"`). Local game data is protected with device-bound SHA-256 checksums and XOR obfuscation.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact Pixel Vector through the developer support email listed on the Google Play Store page.
